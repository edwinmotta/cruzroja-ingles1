Guía Gramatical Médica

1. Formas del Verbo To Be:

• Afirmativo (+): This IS my thermometer. / These ARE our gloves.

• Negativo (-): That IS NOT his blood pressure cuff. / Those ARE NOT her knees.

• Interrogativo (?): IS this your registered nurse? / ARE those their IV lines?

2. Demostrativos (Demonstratives):

  • This: Singular + Cerca (This is my face)
  • That: Singular + Lejos (That is her doctor)
  • These: Plural + Cerca (These are my fingers)
  • Those: Plural + Lejos (Those are their feet)

3. Adjetivos Posesivos (Possessive Adjectives):

Expresan pertenencia antes del objeto o profesión:

my, your, his, her, its, our, their
